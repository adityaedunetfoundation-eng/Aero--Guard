THE AEROGUARD APPROACH

Safety intelligence for the space between aircraft and ground.

We are building an autonomous safety layer for runways, ramps, cargo zones, and every high-consequence environment where a small object can create a large problem.

OUR MISSION

Make the invisible operationally visible.

AeroGuard combines perception, navigation, voice, and telemetry into a system that helps teams notice earlier and act with less hesitation.

The platform is designed as a supervised prototype today, with the architecture to grow toward certified edge deployment tomorrow.
